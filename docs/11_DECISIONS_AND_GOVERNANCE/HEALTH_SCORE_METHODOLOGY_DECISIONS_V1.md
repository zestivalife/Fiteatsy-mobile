# Health Score Methodology Decisions V1

Status: **PROPOSED_NOT_CLINICALLY_VALIDATED**. None of the proposals below authorises a production numeric score. Existing weights remain unchanged. Missing methodology remains fail-closed.

## Shared contract

- Normalised score range: 0–100 only after the relevant decision is approved; cap at both bounds.
- Missing required input: `INSUFFICIENT_DATA`; missing methodology: `METHODOLOGY_PENDING`; explicit non-participation: `NOT_APPLICABLE`.
- Confidence: low below the minimum history, moderate at the minimum, high only with complete current inputs and the approved full window.
- Overall score: remove and renormalise only `NOT_APPLICABLE`; never drop `METHODOLOGY_PENDING`; proposed handling of `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` requires HS-01 approval.
- Health day uses the canonical timezone/wake-day aggregation contract. No legacy `FIT-WELLNESS-200.v1` numeric fallback is permitted.

## Detailed sleep proposal

Duration uses wake-day-attributed asleep minutes against an explicitly selected target. Efficiency is asleep minutes divided by the valid sleep opportunity. Deep and REM remain independent inputs; neither raw minutes nor percentages are scores until age/context ranges are approved. Quality may combine Deep, REM and efficiency only after their individual normalization is approved. Consistency compares local bedtime and wake time using circular-time statistics over an approved window; the proposed operational minimum is seven valid nights, subject to approval. Overlaps are unioned by the canonical sleep-session aggregator. No component is treated as zero merely because it is missing.
