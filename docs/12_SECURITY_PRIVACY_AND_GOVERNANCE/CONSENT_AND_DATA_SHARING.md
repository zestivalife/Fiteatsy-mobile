# Fiteatsy — Consent & Data Sharing

## Distinctions

Consent is not authentication.

Consent is not Practitioner assignment.

Practitioner assignment is not automatically consent for every possible secondary use.

## Consent Records

Where explicit consent is required, a durable record should be capable of storing:

- consent type;
- subject/client;
- policy/version;
- granted/declined status;
- timestamp;
- withdrawal timestamp;
- source/context.

## Consultant Sharing

Professional access should require the approved combination of:

- platform identity;
- product/client relationship;
- CAP-003 authorization;
- applicable consent/policy.

## Withdrawal

Withdrawal semantics must define what stops prospectively and what historical records must remain for legitimate/auditable reasons.

## Legal Guard

Exact legal wording, lawful basis, retention and jurisdiction-specific consent requirements require qualified legal/privacy review.

Codex must not invent legal compliance claims.
