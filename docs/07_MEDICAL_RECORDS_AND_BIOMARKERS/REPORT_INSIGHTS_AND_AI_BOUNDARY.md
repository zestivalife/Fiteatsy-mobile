# Fiteatsy — Report Insights & AI Boundary

## Layered Model

```text
Original Report
      |
      v
Extracted Structured Data
      |
      v
Validated / Normalised Biomarkers
      |
      v
Deterministic Comparison / Rules
      |
      v
Governed Health Context
      |
      v
AI-Assisted Explanation [optional]
```

## AI May Assist With

Subject to CAP-010 governance:

- summarising a report in user-friendly language;
- explaining longitudinal changes;
- organising extracted content;
- generating questions a user may consider discussing with a qualified professional;
- Practitioner workflow summarisation.

## AI Must Not

- invent laboratory values;
- alter source values;
- fabricate reference ranges;
- silently change units;
- claim a diagnosis from insufficient evidence;
- prescribe medication;
- guarantee recovery;
- override Practitioner decisions;
- turn extraction uncertainty into certainty.

## Traceability

Where AI explains report information, the product should retain enough linkage to the underlying structured/source data.

## Model Version

Material AI-generated artifacts should support model/prompt/version provenance where required by CAP-010.

## Clinical Truth

The AI narrative is not the authoritative medical record.
