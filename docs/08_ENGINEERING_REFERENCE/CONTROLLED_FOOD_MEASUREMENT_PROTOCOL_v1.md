# Controlled Indian Food Measurement Protocol v1

Status: candidate methodology. It does not approve a Food or preparation.

## Equipment and calibration

- Use a calibrated digital kitchen scale with resolution recorded for every batch; target resolution is 1 g or better.
- Verify the scale with a traceable reference weight before the session. Record observed value and do not proceed outside the equipment tolerance.
- Place each empty vessel on the scale and tare immediately before weighing.
- Record all values in grams. Volume labels such as katori, cup, or glass are display labels only; the Food-specific filled weight is measured.

## Batch procedure

1. Confirm the preparation ID and exact reviewed formula revision.
2. Record every ingredient Food Version ID and raw edible quantity independently.
3. Record water and oil/fat separately; never infer retained water or oil.
4. Follow the reviewed preparation method and endpoint.
5. Weigh the complete edible prepared batch in a tared vessel and record final weight.
6. Fill the declared serving vessel normally, weigh the Food only, and repeat three times without reusing the previous fill.
7. For piece Foods, weigh at least five independently formed pieces and retain every observation. Do not report a guessed “medium” weight.
8. Record operator, date, equipment ID, scale resolution, and deviations.
9. Submit measurements for automated validation and nutrition calculation.
10. A qualified Nutrition Reviewer approves the exact calculation hash or requests changes.

Missing values, zero/negative final weight, unknown Food Version IDs, unit mismatch, or formula/measurement mismatch fail validation. Editing formula, measurements, ingredient versions, yield, or serving after approval invalidates approval.

## Reference vessels

No universal gram conversion is defined. Each vessel must receive an internal ID, water-volume calibration in millilitres, tare weight, and photograph/reference drawing. Every Food served in that vessel requires its own measured filled weight.
