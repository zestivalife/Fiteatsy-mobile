# India Food Source Policy

Indian-specific foods accept only rights-cleared `INDIA_AUTHORITATIVE` or governed `INDIA_LOCAL_LAB` sources. Foreign national tables are rejected. `GLOBAL_GENERIC_APPROVED` is restricted to non-Indian-specific commodities with exact or approved-equivalent identity, complete required nutrition, reusable rights, and human evidence. Moong Dal and dry Poha remain isolated on the India NABL path.
