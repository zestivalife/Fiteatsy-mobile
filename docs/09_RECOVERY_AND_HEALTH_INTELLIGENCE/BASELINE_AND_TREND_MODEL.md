# Fiteatsy — Baseline & Trend Model

## Baseline

Progress requires something meaningful to compare against.

Potential baseline strategies include:

- initial observation window;
- rolling personal baseline;
- Practitioner-defined programme baseline;
- condition-specific approved baseline.

The strategy must be methodology-specific.

## Personalisation

Where appropriate, longitudinal change against a user's own baseline may be more useful than generic population comparison.

This does not eliminate the need for clinically/product-valid limits where relevant.

## Trend Windows

Possible windows:

- daily;
- 7-day;
- 30-day;
- programme-specific.

Metric-specific aggregation rules apply.

## Noise

Daily health data can be noisy.

The methodology should avoid treating a single transient measurement as a definitive health trajectory unless that use is explicitly valid.

## Biomarkers

Lab tests may occur weeks or months apart.

They should not be forced into the same daily cadence as wearable metrics.

## Recalculation

If late/corrected observations arrive, affected historical derived results may need controlled recalculation with version provenance.
