# Fiteatsy — Explanation & AI Boundary

## AI Role

AI may help translate governed results into understandable language.

Example architecture:

```text
Versioned Calculation
       |
       v
Structured Result
       |
       v
Approved Context
       |
       v
AI Explanation
```

## AI May

- summarise changes;
- explain which tracked dimensions contributed;
- describe data gaps;
- generate user-friendly trend narratives;
- help Practitioners review longitudinal context.

## AI Must Not

- invent measurements;
- create the authoritative recovery score;
- change deterministic calculations;
- fabricate causes;
- diagnose from insufficient evidence;
- prescribe treatment;
- guarantee improvement/recovery.

## Causal Language

Prefer language reflecting observed association/trend.

Do not state that an intervention caused an improvement merely because both occurred in the same period.

## Provenance

Generated explanations should reference the structured result/context from which they were generated.
